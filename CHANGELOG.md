# Changelog

## 0.9.0

First public release, for Foundry VTT V14. Playable, but new: expect rough edges.

- Scoundrel sheet laid out like the paper playbooks, with a Load tab; Ghost, Hull and Vampire supported.
- Crew sheet with the clickable claims map (and prison claims), upgrades, cohorts, and a Score & Downtime tab that follows the order of play.
- Action, resistance, fortune, engagement, group action and flashback rolls; assist, push and Devil's Bargain; stress, trauma and desperate xp applied automatically; the GM can call for a roll with position and effect set.
- Payoff and heat, entanglements, downtime activities (with coin to raise results), crew development and end-of-session xp.
- Clocks as actors with a clocks panel; faction sheets and a faction ledger.
- Compendia: playbooks, special abilities, items, crew types, crew abilities, the factions of Doskvol and a rules reference.
- Action ratings show four dots and stop at 3 until the crew has Mastery; ability boxes match the printed sheets.
